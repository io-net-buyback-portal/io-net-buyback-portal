ABOUT IO

Built for decentralized compute.

IO NETWORK

Distributed GPU infrastructure for modern workloads.

io.net is a decentralized computing network designed to aggregate distributed GPU resources and make computing capacity available through a unified infrastructure layer.

The ecosystem connects compute providers and users through a network economy built around accessible GPU capacity.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is io.net?

io.net is a decentralized computing network focused on aggregating distributed GPU resources for AI and machine-learning workloads.

What is the IO token?

$IO is the native token associated with the IO ecosystem and supports economic activity across the network.

What does the calculator do?

It estimates an IO amount from the entered BNB amount using the current displayed market prices and the configured 11% allocation bonus.

What are the participation limits?

This portal is configured with a minimum of 5 BNB and a maximum of 500 BNB.
